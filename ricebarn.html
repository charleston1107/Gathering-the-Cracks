Rice barn

This rice barn has no difference with the “lumbung” of the global south:

they both dry barns for storage and dispersal; they both help local farmers to sell their produce; and they both collect surplus food so as to ensure a smoother transition in adverse situations

↓

It has also witnessed the most communist way of working in the past: all were for the purpose of being pragmatic and productive. Ironically, the rice barn will soon become a cultural and creative park serving coffee, one of the most petite bourgeois and pretentious places we can think of.

What’s even more irritating is that the two gigantic grain silos will remain intact, as natural spectacles or a landmark of the upcoming coffee park.

We maintain a neutral attitude towards the establishment of the coffee park

but after two or three decades, if people only know that the two grain silos represent the coffee park, some spirit of local solidarity will fade away forever
