This bridge looks like any other old Chinese stone bridge you can see in any other tourist attraction sites. But who can imagine that just 30 years ago, it was still an inseparable part for all the people living on the street?

It is true that the bridge has stood there for over a century, but children would climb up the bridge to fly kites; adults would have almost every meal on it, chatting with each other, and the elderly would bring stools to play chess and sip tea

It was meant to be nothing unusual, but also a significant part of the lives of the people in the past, as a living history constantly serving generations after generations

Now, tourists walk on it with the feeling of experiencing a fancy legend, but never supposing it as a part of quotidian life.

We really want to ask here: is the “方法“ (method) of cultural preservation simply just to isolate people from the past?
