Certain things should be remembered…

The celluloid-colored shadows and the riverbank

In the breakfast shop the handmade noodles

Homemade wine, sealed in clay jars with mud

The clean streets; the water

People sit on the curb, talking

Fish scales scattered along the roadside like falling snow

 

应当记住某些事物…

赛璐珞色的阴影与水岸

面店铺早餐 擀面

笼中的公鸡啼鸣
家中自酿的酒，泥坛封口

洁净的街道 水

马路牙子上坐着攀谈

路边/河边到处都是鱼鳞 雪花一般
